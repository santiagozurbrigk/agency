THE DIAGNOSIS
It is never one big mistake.
It is a dozen small leaks.

These are the four constraints that quietly compress margin until there is nothing left to scale with.

Low AOV

No bundles. No upsells. No post-purchase sequences. You're shipping orders that barely break even and building zero margin on every sale.

Creative Burnout

One winning ad carries everything. When it dies, and it always does, you're back to zero with no system for finding the next one.

No Retention System

One-time buyers and no way to bring them back. No LTV strategy, no retention layer, no recurring revenue. You're refilling a leaking bucket every single month.

Margin Bleed

You're not going to see a crash coming. You'll just watch margins compress month after month until there's nothing left to scale with.
