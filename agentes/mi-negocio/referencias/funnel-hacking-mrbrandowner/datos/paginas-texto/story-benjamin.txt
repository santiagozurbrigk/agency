Benjamin DM'd me in January. He and his brothers had been running a supplement brand for over a year. It made money, but it couldn't hold $10K days, whatever they tried.

I looked at what they wanted to scale and told them straight: I wasn't confident in it. I could have taken the money and pushed it anyway. I'd rather they win, so we picked a new hero product with real room to scale.

I was full at the time. They waited for a start date. When it came, we went to work.
