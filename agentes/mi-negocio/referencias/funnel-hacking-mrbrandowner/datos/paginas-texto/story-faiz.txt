WHERE HE STARTED

Seven years ago Faiz started ecom as a side hustle. His first win was a hijab brand he and a friend bought for $1,000. They packed the orders themselves in his office, the same office he works from today.

They handed the ads to an agency. When ROAS dropped, the agency told him they were "talking to Facebook about it". That was when he decided to learn every skill himself before paying anyone to do it. He's put six figures into his own education since.

BUILDING WITH NO SAFETY NET

Selling into the US from Pakistan means no PayPal, no credit cards, and banks that close your account overnight. Every dollar of ad spend comes out of his own pocket. For a long stretch he didn't pay himself at all.

He launched his supplement brand in January and lost money: $2K in, $300 back. In February the ads clicked. He went from 0 to 300K a month in three months, then to $1.5M a month with three people in the office.

WHAT HE LEARNED FROM ME AND REMO

Faiz can make ads. He came to us with one question: the brand was doing 2.5 ROAS on the front end, so why did every morning feel like a dead end?

The answer was the backend.

•
Supply. In July his supplier let him down and he lost around 100K worth of subscriptions. We sorted the supplier and set him up with US fulfilment.

•
Retention. We built cancellation flows and a post-purchase sequence of about 30 emails. It shows customers social proof and what changes in their day once the product works. That's what took churn down.

•
Front end. Remo worked on creative diversity, copy, angles, formats, landing pages and CRO.

•
People. We connected him with operators and the people who can fix an ad account problem in a day. Some of them are now his closest friends in the space.

With all that handled, getting from 30K days to 100K days came down to volume. More creative strategists, more editors. The team now has capacity for 20 briefs a day, three ads each.

When I asked what working with us changed, he said it in one word: everything.

WHAT HE'D TELL YOU

Go through your best ads and work out which emotions they make people feel, then recreate those yourself. Do the manual work before you hire. Write down why you expect each ad to work before it launches. Ad spend is only wasted when you learn nothing from it.
