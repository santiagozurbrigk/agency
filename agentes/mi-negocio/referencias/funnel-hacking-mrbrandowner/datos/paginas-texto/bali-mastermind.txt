WHY THIS EXISTS
Every month starts at zero.

That's the trap. You find a winner, it prints, competitors pull the creative out of the ads library and run it in three countries, CPAs climb, the winner fades, and you're hunting again. Brandon ran that business for six years — over 8 million euros with a one-time product — and still woke up every morning checking whether the ROAS had held.

This room is about the other version: a product that runs out, a customer who gets billed again, and a month that opens with revenue already on the board. Seventy-five brand owners, Brandon and Remo, three days, and the whole machine taken apart in front of you with the numbers open. No livestream. No replay. No recording.

What gets said in this room stays in this room.
